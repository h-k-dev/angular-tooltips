Same API on both engines

show(), hide(), toggle() and [hkTooltipDisabled] behave identically. Both targets bind a ticking counter — hover one and watch the open tooltip re-render in place every second. (A JS tooltip opened with toggle() closes on the next click anywhere else, exactly like tippy's hideOnClick; a manually opened invoker tooltip stays until hide().)
